# 0.5.2 rc2 changes and playtest

This candidate follows the [rc1 checklist](0.5.2-rc1-playtest.md). It contains the November 12 bug fixes. Local acceptance is complete: 625 unit cases, 276 native mod cases, 32 selected graphical entrypoints, multiplayer, sixteen MOVE and ten BUILD crash/restart checkpoints pass with retained results and affected fixture corrections. Publication through release CI is pending.

The candidate tag is `v0.5.2_rc2`. The in-game version remains `0.5.2`; use the release filename to distinguish the candidate.

## Changes

- The Ship Controller has a blank line below its tabs and above Fly manually / View ship / Close all doors. Trip offers Undock when it is the available departure action. Continue journey still undocks and resumes a paid journey in one action.
- Encounter cores explain whether a vessel can be claimed. Claim vessel is the main action on an unclaimed derelict. The page explains that the previous ship stays put and that claiming does not repair or restock the prize. Claiming finishes its ownership save before opening the new Ship Controller.
- The approved Scanner map keeps its full width, has a black background and uses 18 rows. Search and View share one row. Names stay beside contacts; nearby labels and connecting dots handle crowded positions. View includes stations, ships, planets, pads and berth details.
- Landing turns the physical hull so its main entrance faces the Pad Computer's side of the ring. The computer block's facing and the ship's bow do not choose the arrival direction. The rotated footprint must fit before the move starts. Occupants, directional blocks, cargo, saved air, devices, computer disks, beds and recovery data follow the same move.
- Generated raised pads have foundations down to terrain. Their stairs continue until they meet ground rather than ending after nine steps. Planet-pad foundations no longer stop after 24 blocks.
- Newly generated ships draw optional room uses from one shared bag across all decks. Opposite rooms can have different uses. A use repeats after the other uses that fit the space have been used. Medium and large twin and dome ships have two and three connected decks. Space beside stairwells becomes rooms, and upper spine decks retain their side rooms. Stair supports join the hull while keeping headroom clear.

Existing ships and pads are not rebuilt to apply generation changes. Use a newly generated craft or landing site to review those corrections.

## Playtest

### Controls and Scanner

- [ ] Open the Ship Controller. Check the two blank lines and that tab contents, help text and buttons do not overlap.
- [ ] Dock without an available course. Check that Trip offers Undock. With a paused paid journey, check that Continue journey still handles departure and resume.
- [ ] Open the Scanner and back out to Stars. Check the black map fills the available width and has more vertical space.
- [ ] Select contacts near each other and near the edges. Check their names remain readable and labels select the corresponding contact.
- [ ] Open View with the mouse and keyboard. Switch between categories; check search, pagination and Set destination still work. Dismiss the menu without accidentally selecting the item below it.
- [ ] Select a star twice to browse its planets. Set a destination, complete a journey and select another destination.

### Encounter ownership

- [ ] Visit an unclaimed derelict. Read the ownership explanation and claim it. Check that its Ship Controller opens after the save completes.
- [ ] Check that the prize keeps its damage, cargo and fuel. Check that your previous ship remains where you left it and that you can return to it.
- [ ] Visit a public station that cannot be claimed. Check that its core explains this rather than offering station construction controls to a visitor.

### Arrival orientation and saved state

- [ ] Land at pads with their computers on different ring edges. The main entrance should face the computer's edge each time. Include a ship whose bow faces a different direction from its entrance.
- [ ] Land a rectangular ship on a pad whose dimensions fit after turning. Try one that does not fit; refusal should leave the original hull and cargo intact.
- [ ] Exit through the entrance, return aboard, launch and land again. Check that the ship does not drift or keep turning on repeated arrivals.
- [ ] Check paired chests, signs, stairs, doors, beds, directional equipment and decorated item frames. Check cargo counts and battery charge.
- [ ] On an airless destination, check that landing does not refill the sealed rooms. Open a door and check air still redistributes normally.
- [ ] With another player, travel with crew and a mounted passenger. Include an offline crew member, then have them reconnect. Check location and facing.
- [ ] Set a bed respawn, land after turning, and test respawning aboard. Also repeat Store and Deploy and check the entrance orientation and title cycle.

### Generated ships and pads

- [ ] Generate fresh examples from each ship family. Walk every deck and the stairs, including a medium and a large twin and dome.
- [ ] Check room variety. Look for repeated uses before the fitting alternatives have appeared. Compare the actual furnishings and usable walking space, not just the room names.
- [ ] Inspect the rooms beside stairwells and the top deck of a large spine ship. Check the former solid areas are usable.
- [ ] Find a newly generated raised pad on uneven ground. Walk its stairs all the way down without jumping or falling. Check the foundations do not leave an open shaded cavity underneath.

## Still deferred

Performance measurements remain stopped. Tiny starter layouts, planetary habs, plants consuming oxygen, sleeping through FTL, automatic multi-hop travel and additional effects remain separate feature requests. This candidate does not claim to implement them.

When reporting a failure, include the candidate filename, ship design, location, the action you took, the visible message and whether the problem affects an existing or newly generated build.
