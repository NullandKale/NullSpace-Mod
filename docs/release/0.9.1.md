# NullSpace 0.9.1

Fixes the two problems found playing 0.9.0 (pressure plates that stuck down, and orbital stations nobody could use), starts new worlds as a crash landing on Terra, lists nearby places the same way at the helm and the Scanner, and makes landing fly close to the planet first.

Existing worlds do not need a reset. A server with no start setting keeps the ordinary Minecraft start for its new players.

## Changes

- **Pressure plates and buttons no longer stick.** A ship's area refuses block changes while it jumps, moves or docks, and a plate's own tick reschedules only while something presses it, so a plate stepped off during a jump stayed down for good with its door open. A plate or button the lock keeps down now tries again every half second until the lock ends ([`TransferLocks.retryRelease`](../../src/main/java/com/nullandkale/nullspace/gameplay/travel/TransferLocks.java)).
- **Stations nobody owns are open.** A station the world generated around a planet, like a derelict, now lets any visitor open, use, break and build anything ([`StationAccess.unowned`](../../src/main/java/com/nullandkale/nullspace/gameplay/station/StationAccess.java)). A player's own station stays protected. See [station reference](../design/station-reference.md).
- **New worlds start crash-landed on Terra** in the tiny Rivet Spark ([`WorldStartSettings.NEW_WORLD`](../../src/main/java/com/nullandkale/nullspace/gameplay/start/WorldStartSettings.java)). Servers keep the ordinary start unless set. See [space start](../design/space-start.md).
- **No more spawning in the wrong place first.** A dark "Preparing your ship" screen covers the world while a new player's start is built, until they stand in the ship ([`StartScreen`](../../src/client/java/com/nullandkale/nullspace/ui/screen/StartScreen.java)).
- **Every start leaves the bridge with air.** The generator nearest the helm and the air supply that feeds the bridge are never broken by a crash, on any planet ([`CrashDamage`](../../src/main/java/com/nullandkale/nullspace/gameplay/start/CrashDamage.java)). A random planet in orbit no longer warns about a far-away star.
- **The helm and the Scanner list the same places.** The helm's Trip tab lists the places at the current planet (pads, stations and ships, and its orbit when landed or docked); one click chooses and Engage goes. The Scanner lists the same rows first, then this system's planets (each opens in place), then the star map. See [ship navigation](../design/ship-navigation.md#the-ship-controller).
- **Terminal screens react to the mouse.** The control under the mouse lights up, a grey control says why it is unavailable, the wheel scrolls lists, and a double click sets a destination.
- **Landing flies close first.** The ship turns to the planet and flies near it, burning fuel like docking, before the landing transfer ([`PlanetApproach`](../../src/main/java/com/nullandkale/nullspace/gameplay/navigation/PlanetApproach.java)). With no fuel, **Emergency landing** (confirmed by a second press) crash-lands on the pad: engines, generators and air supplies break and the hull tears, and the bridge keeps its air. See [landing flies first](../design/ship-navigation.md#landing-flies-first).
- **Field guide pages** describe the new controls.

## Tests and tooling

- New native tests cover held-down plates and buttons, the place lists, and a real approach and emergency landing; the docking, start and navigation graphical tests use the new screens.
- A banner across the top of every graphical test window ([`TestBanner`](../../src/client/java/com/nullandkale/nullspace/dev/TestBanner.java)) names the running test, its elapsed time and the line it waits at.
