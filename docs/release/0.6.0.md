# 0.6.0 changes and playtest

0.6.0 is the first release of planned stations and ships. It follows the [0.5.2 rc3 checklist](0.5.2-rc3-playtest.md). Existing ships and stations in a saved world are not rebuilt, except as noted below.

Local verification before tagging: all 676 unit cases, every native server test, all graphical entrypoints, the multiplayer check, the MOVE and BUILD crash checkpoints and the planet restart check pass. Performance measurements remain deferred, and the manual playthrough below has not been done.

## Changes

- **Planned stations.** Stations are planned from a brief (maker, purpose, size, seed): a ring of rooms, halls and stair halls over one to four decks, with berths for full-size ships. Rooms are bigger than before (small 8 to 12 blocks, large 12 to 20), floors are two blocks thick, interior doors open on pressure plates, and logs run floor to ceiling. Small stations are about 45 by 30 blocks, medium about 55 by 50 and large about 95 by 100.
- **Furnished rooms.** About 25 room designs (kitchen, workshop, foundry, cargo hold, growing bay, garden, lab, archive, observation, cabin, bunk room, infirmary, brig and more) with item frames, space-suit stands, paintings and animals. Containers in a working room stay empty.
- **Orbital stations.** A tenth of the bodies in star systems and half of the nebulae, black holes, rogue planets, pulsars and asteroid fields have a planned station in orbit, and Sol's home orbit always has one. The old Waypoint Station and Manufacturer Exchange are gone. `/nullspace station orbital` lists the zones that have one, and `/nullspace station new <maker> <purpose> <size>` builds one beside your ship.
- **Planned ships.** The six full-size catalog ships (Rivet Bolt, Hauler and Ranger; Halcyon Swift, Courier and Horizon; Bastion Ward, Tender and Pioneer) are laid out the same way, in small, medium and large sizes with large and small rooms and mirrored pairs along a spine. Small ships are about 23 by 29 blocks, medium 25 by 31 and large 27 by 32, so a pad needs to be at least that wide. Tiny ships are unchanged. `/nullspace shipyard random` builds a gallery of them.
- **Derelict loot.** A found vessel stocks one chest in every room from that room's own table, tiered by danger, with fuel, worn goods, vanilla blocks and finds that improve with danger. A history (evacuated, breach, power failure or interrupted work) nudges the tiers by at most one. Ships you buy, your starter ship and working stations stay empty.
- **Journey stations.** The abandoned-station encounter builds a planned, furnished derelict station.
- **Creative System Upgrade.** `nullspace:creative_system_upgrade` (no recipe; `/give` it) makes a Scanner tier 4 and adds spawn tools to its screen: spawn a star system, or an encounter of a chosen kind and danger, as a jump destination.
- **Power and air.** A planned vessel's doors are powered before its air, and its power plant grows with its rooms.

## Playtest

- [ ] Fly to Sol's home orbit station. Dock, board, and walk the rooms. Check the pressure-plate doors, stairs and furnishing.
- [ ] Run `/nullspace station orbital`, fly to a listed zone and dock at its station. Repeat for a small, medium and large station.
- [ ] Buy each catalog ship from a beacon. Check that the pad size message is right, then launch, jump, dock and land. Check the bridge, engines and rooms.
- [ ] Find a derelict ship and a derelict station. Open the chest in several rooms and check the loot against the danger of the zone.
- [ ] Take a Creative System Upgrade (`/give @s nullspace:creative_system_upgrade`) and put it in a Scanner. Spawn a star system and an encounter, jump to each and check the buttons are laid out cleanly.
- [ ] Run `/nullspace version` and check it reports `v0.6.0`.

## Still deferred

The derelict damage pass is not built: a derelict's history changes its loot but not its blocks, so derelict stations and ships are not yet wrecked. Station claiming, player-built stations on the new planner and performance measurements remain unbuilt. A few ships' rooms may feel cramped at ship size; report any that do.
