# 0.6.1 changes and playtest

0.6.1 follows [0.6.0](0.6.0.md). It adds wrecked derelicts, tougher and more varied crew, the stowaway creatures and a test gallery of every enemy. Existing ships and stations in a saved world are not rebuilt, except that a derelict that has not been boarded yet gets the new damage and crew when it is first visited.

## Changes

- **Derelict damage.** A found ship or station is no longer intact. A pass over the built vessel, driven by its danger and history, collapses ceilings, breaches the hull, breaks floors, cracks and darkens rooms, jams or opens doors and takes out power. The dock room, the controller and the loot chests are left alone, and rooms the pass leaves whole keep their air. Station claiming is switched off in game (the code is kept).
- **Tougher crew.** Lost crew are zombies and skeletons in the space suit, with no name tags. Each goes through vanilla's own spawn step with a difficulty set by the site's danger, so armor, enchantments, health and speed bonuses and zombie leaders all apply. Wherever vanilla would give armor the crew wears the armored suit, with an extra chance that grows with danger (about 20 percent of pieces at danger 1, 45 at 2 and 75 at 3). They never break doors, which would vent a room.
- **Ranks.** At danger 3 the first zombie is a zombie villager and the first skeleton a wither skeleton (where its height fits); a skeleton in a room with no air is a stray.
- **Time aboard.** While a player is aboard, more crew turn up out of sight every two minutes, up to two per danger level.
- **Stowaways.** Planetside animals that came aboard in cargo and adapted, each with its own entity type so the vanilla creatures look the same on planets: pale spiders, pale cave spiders and pale silverfish (danger 1 and up), the ghost creeper, grey and half transparent (danger 2 and up), and the violet slime and violet blaze (danger 3). One stowaway at danger 1, two at 2 and three at 3, and one reinforcement in three.
- **Enemy gallery.** `/nullspace shipyard enemies` builds a lit hall with every crew rank, the repair bot and every stowaway at every danger, standing still, for review.
- **Creative builder.** The creative Scanner can choose every setting of an encounter (kind, danger, history, seed) and Start FTL goes to a temporary encounter zone.
- **Loot.** The top end of the loot sits in the treasure chest only, with tiers per vessel.
- **Tether.** The ship's air line no longer reaches inside a found derelict, so a breached room is dangerous without a canister.

## Playtest

- [ ] Run `/nullspace shipyard enemies` and walk the hall. Check every row at every danger, the ghost creeper's transparency and the white spiders.
- [ ] Walk a danger 3 derelict from the dock. Check the wrecked rooms, the armored crew, the zombie villager and wither skeleton, and that a breached room is dangerous (no air, no tether).
- [ ] Stay aboard a derelict for several minutes and check that reinforcements arrive out of sight and stop at the cap.
- [ ] Look at a vanilla creeper, spider, slime and blaze on a planet and in the Nether and check they look vanilla.
- [ ] Use the creative Scanner builder to start FTL to an encounter with chosen settings.
- [ ] Run `/nullspace version` and check it reports `v0.6.1`.

## Still deferred

The violet slime and blaze are also meant for the warp core, a custom beacon built from colored glass that powers a station and needs a nether star; its design is not settled. Player-built stations on the new planner, claiming and performance measurements remain unbuilt.
