# NullSpace 0.9.0

Gives the game its own sound, docks ships hull to hull so the crew walk from one vessel into the other, and makes jumps seamless: no loading screen, only a short flash while the sky changes.

Existing worlds do not need a reset. Ships docked in an older save stay docked as before, with collars and boarding through the door, until they undock.

## Changes

- **Docked hulls stand side by side.** A ship that clamps on moves beside its berth behind the loading screen, and a short docking arm joins the two docking doors. Open the door and walk across: walking into the other vessel makes you its crew, and walking back returns you. Each hull keeps its own air, and the arm is breathable and within the tether. Undocking, an emergency undock included, first moves the hull back to an area of its own with its crew, then backs away as before. A berth set too deep in its station for an arm (more than 31 blocks), or with another vessel in the way, docks the old way, and so does a dock whose hulls cannot be joined after three tries. See [docking](../design/space-encounters-and-docking.md#two-hulls-one-walk).
- **Sound.** A small synthesizer ([`SynthVoice`](../../src/main/java/com/nullandkale/nullspace/model/sound/SynthVoice.java)) plays named cues from [`cues.json`](../../src/main/resources/assets/nullspace/synth/cues.json): a low hum aboard any hull in space, a jolt with each jump and a drone while cruising, engines that hum with thrust, docking clamps and releases and maneuvering jets, weapon and turret shots and hits on the hull, a hiss or rush at a breach, and an Air Supply refilling a room. Sound follows the hull: nothing carries through vacuum outside a hull, a hull muffles sound on worlds with air, and closed doors and walls muffle sound between rooms. Minecraft's own sounds follow the same rules as far as its sound engine allows. See [sound](../design/sound.md).
- **Air warnings that stay out of the way.** Inside a ship, breathing from your suit plays only a quiet chime every few seconds. The low-air alarm, a steady beep, sounds only when you are suffocating (oxygen below 20%), anywhere. A suited player on the ship's air line rests at 20% and hears the chime, not the alarm. A breach has no klaxon: its hiss says where it is. No sound in the game wails like a siren or buzzes.
- **The Synth block.** A craftable ship device with its own screen: set a tone and noise, an envelope and a pulse, play it once or as a loop, from redstone or from any ship terminal (`ship synth`). It needs the ship's controls powered.
- **Seamless jumps.** A jump in space (entering FTL, a signal, resuming, arriving) happens on the next tick with no loading screen: each crew member sees a short flash while their sky changes and keeps moving. Open screens, the helm terminal included, stay open.
- **The scanner charts places before anyone visits them.** It lists a region's planned stations and derelicts, and planet landing pads and the Terra Harbor before they are built; choosing a charted pad builds it.
- **No rail spikes.** Station, hab and ship builds and wreck damage remove iron bars and glass panes that join nothing, so railings end cleanly.
- **Clean site storage.** A world-generated site clears leftover entities (item frames from an earlier site) from its storage area before it places its first block.

## Commands

- `/nullspace shipyard sounds` builds a sound gallery ([`SoundGallery`](../../src/main/java/com/nullandkale/nullspace/gameplay/shipyard/SoundGallery.java)): every sound the game makes on a stand with a button or lever, the live values the game uses side by side, the Minecraft sounds the game borrows, and a row heard through doors and walls.
- `/nullspace debug holding` ([`HoldingView`](../../src/main/java/com/nullandkale/nullspace/gameplay/scene/HoldingView.java)) shows the holding dimension where hulls really stand, as a spectator; run it again to go back as you were.
- `/nullspace sound <cue>` lets anyone hear one cue for themselves.

## Tests and tooling

- A test hull docks at a station for every delivered ship design: it joins the berth, the player walks across and back, it separates and backs away, and the clamp and release are heard only once the hull has finished moving.
- The graphical docking tests walk the arm with real input, clicking both doors and holding forward, and check that the client hears the clamp and release where the player is.
- [`SoundCuesTest`](../../src/test/java/com/nullandkale/nullspace/model/sound/SoundCuesTest.java) holds every looping cue to a steady, smooth tone. Further tests cover hearing, the air sounds, the Synth block, the gallery and the holding view.
