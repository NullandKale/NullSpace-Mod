# 0.7.0 changes and playtest

0.7.0 follows [0.6.1](0.6.1.md). It adds the crash-landing start, a menu for choosing the ship and planet, flight with momentum, engine exhaust that responds to thrust, and a crew of space mobs on planets with no air. Existing worlds keep their start; the crash start is chosen when a world is created.

Release CI [passed](https://github.com/NullandKale/NullSpace-Mod/actions/runs/43782992595) and published [v0.7.0](https://github.com/NullandKale/NullSpace-Mod/releases/tag/v0.7.0) as a full release. The downloaded `nullspace-0.7.0.jar` matches SHA-256 `ee63fa577e188e28e00a81d7c31dab9cee0d6d5247d9ffefb829384e620062e9`. It is not yet installed on the playtest server or in the Modrinth testing profile.

Local verification before tagging, with `parallel-test.ps1 -Graphics -ServerWorkers 2 -GraphicsWorkers 4`: 717 unit cases, all server tests (two shards) and all 43 graphical entrypoints pass, and the content assets match their generator. The multiplayer, crash, build-crash and planet-restart scripts each pass on their own. The manual playtest below has not been done. Natural spawning of the airless-planet mobs has not been watched in play; the tests place the mobs by hand.

## Changes

- **Crash-landing start.** Land a chosen ship on a chosen planet, wrecked: explosions around the pad, 35 percent of the ring gone, a third of the machines swapped for broken variants (wrecks that drop 40 percent of what the machine cost), a broken Pad Computer and every NullSpace recipe unlocked. The player wakes facing the damage with 16 bread, the suit and the field guide (and an Air Canister where there is no air). Design: [crash-start.md](../design/crash-start.md).
- **Single or multi crash.** Single crash: the first player crashes and everyone later wakes in the same ship. Multi crash: each player has a ship at the nearest free site on a random or chosen planet. Joining never fails; a failed start falls back to the planet's surface.
- **Starting ship and planet menu.** Pick the ship and a planet type (random, Terra, desert, ice, lava, ocean, jungle, barren or toxic), with warnings and a difficulty that reflects air, trees and ship size. Design: [starting-planet.md](../design/starting-planet.md).
- **Stone, wood and metal on every planet.** Water, lava, sapling and dirt hang in the cabin; every space mob spawns on airless planets and drops iron, copper, redstone and glass when a player kills it.
- **Flight physics.** Ships have mass, momentum and an assist that damps drift (G toggles it); an unpiloted ship keeps drifting and braking; closing the helm no longer stops the ship. Design: [flight-physics.md](../design/flight-physics.md).
- **Engine exhaust.** Larger particles that follow thrust and speed, colored by engine tier, and drawn in the helm view with Minecraft's own particle sprites.

## Playtest

- [ ] Create a world with a crash start on Terra, an airless planet and a lava planet; check the cabin frames, the wreck smoke and that stone, wood, copper and iron can be had.
- [ ] Join a single-crash world with a second player and a multi-crash world with two.
- [ ] Kill zombies, skeletons and stowaways on an airless planet and check the drops and that they spawn naturally.
- [ ] Fly with assist on and off and watch the exhaust in the helm view.
- [ ] Run `/nullspace version` and check it reports `v0.7.0`.
