# Pad resizing and planetary weather — 2026-10-26

Completed ring edits now refresh a registered pad automatically. A complete inner ring keeps defining the old pad until it is removed. Once the larger ring and dirt/grass interior are valid, registration keeps the same ID, name, and controller and publishes the new footprint. An incomplete ring or overlapping registration does not replace the last valid footprint; normal inspection reports the construction problem.

Floor edits coalesce for five ticks, and background processing handles at most one dirty pad per tick. A rotating background scan catches already saved edits and chunk reloads. Ship-controller lookup also refreshes nearby candidate pads before choosing one, so an existing World 5-style save does not need another manual registration. Refresh never loads remote chunks. The existing requirement still applies: the pad computer must stand on the ring belonging to that pad.

Landed ship allocations also grow with the registered pad. If the allocation origin changes, device positions, guest-computer storage addresses, monitor selections, finite-air cells, and offline crew offsets are translated into the new local frame while retaining physical world positions. Device IDs, battery energy, computer files, and crew orientation remain intact. Air falling outside the maximum allocation is counted as construction venting. Ships already in space retain their physical allocation, and active travel prevents a concurrent ground-allocation edit.

Planetary atmosphere shading now retains brighter HDR sunlight on clear days. Rain blends the sky toward an overcast appearance and obscures the sun, planets, stars, and distant gas; thunderstorms darken it further, including appropriate night values. Weather comes from the live viewed client dimension, and changing weather rejects stale temporal history. Airless moons and space ignore planetary weather and the atmospheric sun boost. Native terrain, clouds, and precipitation continue to use Minecraft's rendering.

Regression coverage includes automatic expansion with a surviving inner ring, published bounds and landed-ship allocation growth, persistence, invalid construction, stale saved registration repair on interaction, and lossless resource/address translation. Real GPU assertions cover sun brightness, rain, thunder, night, airless views, weather-history invalidation, live client weather, and isolation from another dimension's weather.
