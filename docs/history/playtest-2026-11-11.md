# November 11 playtest

A fresh-world playtest of version 0.5.1. The newer working-tree fixes are not evidence that these reports are resolved in that version. Continue the feature freeze: fix bugs, balance existing content and simplify existing flows; record new features for later.

This follows the [November 10 playtest](playtest-2026-11-10.md). Implementation status and the remaining journal work are in [journal completion work](../development/journal-completion-work.md).

## Features recorded for after the freeze

- **Tiny ships.** Add genuinely tiny layouts, including a bridge-and-engine-room starter. Tiny ships should appear through beacons or player starts, not ordinary random population. Keep the existing medium, large and huge range.
- **Planetary habs.** Extend station construction and life support to surface buildings. Canisters remain useful for EVA; permanent buildings need their own hab system.
- **Low-oxygen crops.** Pop growing plants out of tilled soil below a proposed oxygen threshold of about 20%. Combine the rules with the earlier oxygen-consumption request; the threshold and affected crops are not settled.
- **FTL jolt.** Make entering FTL feel like a strong impulse.
- **Air particles.** Show airflow more often, with opacity tied to movement strength, including gentle room filling from roughly 80% to 100%.
- **Thruster particles.** Show working thrusters visually.
- **Arrival cue.** Add an FTL-completion bell or equivalent clear cue. No bell call was found in the current arrival flow.
- **Automatic multi-hop trips and seamless space transitions.** Plan routes, align at each step and optionally finish with landing. Preserve real regional stops, encounters and other players at those stops. Keep journey duration; reduce repetitive clicks and space-only loading interruptions. Landing may keep its loading transition.
- **Fuel-economy redesign.** Revisit the availability and role of coal/charcoal and the relationship between fuel and batteries. The excessive random-vessel loot is a current balance bug; new propulsion/resource mechanics are deferred.

The earlier reset command, sleeping through FTL and plant oxygen consumption remain recorded in the November 10 feature list.

The per-planet dimension requirement was initially classified as a feature. The user corrected that classification: it is PT11-16 in the current bug-fix scope, together with making nongas planets landable. It is distinct from PT11-5's physical landing-pad obstruction.

## Current behavior behind the questions

- Batteries store electrical power and discharge when generation is insufficient. Jumps separately require generator fuel.
- Space-to-FTL and FTL-to-space change the scene while retaining the space dimension and the ship's physical storage location. The current overlay waits for scene synchronization; it does not represent another dimension transfer.
- Travel validates safe cabins. Engage seals exterior exits while leaving interior doors open for useful air redistribution; Close all doors remains an explicit control.
- Engage sequences an existing trip. Automatic routing across several intermediate regions and seamless presentation are additional work, not the same feature as Continue journey after an encounter.

## What should stay

Keep the FTL waiting time, optional encounters, regional stops and chances to meet other players. Keep room-to-room air movement, canisters for EVA and the lava hazard after landing. Fix the excessive fuel supplies and unclear controls without flattening those parts of play.
